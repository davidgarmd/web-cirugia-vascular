# Surface brief: portada y páginas de patología

Scope: whole public site (home first, then condition pages inherit). Visitor mode: Persuade (home), Read (condition pages).
Audience/job: adult patients from Ciudad Real province, often 60+, on a phone; find what their symptom is, whether it is urgent, and call or WhatsApp.
Proof/content: specialist title, hospital affiliation, eco-doppler in the same visit, focus on chronic venous insufficiency and pelvic venous congestion. No testimonials, ratings or statistics.
Constraints: brand palette and semantic colors (PRODUCT.md), no em-dashes, no eyebrows, no card grid as page structure, rasters only from the brand illustration style.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a hospital wayfinding system. The visitor names what they feel and a coloured floor line, drawn as an open vessel with flow, leads them to their page. It refuses the category default: teal clinic hero, stock doctor photo, grid of service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Clean signage ground (cool off-white), navy #0A2463 directory panels with white sign lettering, light blue #56CCF2 route lines, red #EB5757 only on the urgency route, green #27AE60 only on the habits route. Montserrat 800 as sign lettering at large sizes, Inter for reading. One-stroke pictograms, 10px panel radius, route lines 12px with round caps and a moving flow dash.

STORY: The visitor recognises their symptom, sees in one glance if it is "pide cita" or "urgencias", follows the line to a clear explanation, and calls.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left half: sign-style headline "Cirujano vascular en Ciudad Real", one line of subtext (≤20 words), Llamar and WhatsApp. Right half: a navy directory totem listing 5 symptoms with arrows; from each row a coloured route line exits the panel and runs down the page. One row is red (pierna hinchada de golpe: urgencias). A brand flat-editorial illustration of a patient following a floor line sits at the base.

FORM: hospital wayfinding signage, candidate 6 of 7 on the grounded list, seed key a0578fdf (degraded roll, no challengers).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
