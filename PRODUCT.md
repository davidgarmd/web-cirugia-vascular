# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS generated by a Python script (`contenido.py` + `build.py` → `public/`). No framework, no client-side dependencies. Confirmed by the user.

## Users

- Primary: adults from Ciudad Real (capital and province: Puertollano, Valdepeñas, Tomelloso, Alcázar de San Juan, Manzanares, Daimiel...) with leg symptoms (heaviness, varicose veins, pain when walking, wounds that do not heal), often 45-80 years old, frequently reading on a phone. They search Google for a condition or for "cirujano vascular Ciudad Real" and want to know whether to book, and with whom.
- Secondary: women with chronic pelvic pain, vulvar or pelvic varicose veins, or leg varices that recur after treatment (pelvic venous congestion), who have often seen several specialists without an answer.
- Secondary: relatives booking for older parents; patients with diabetes and foot wounds; people with an incidental "dilated aorta" or carotid plaque in a report.
- Job: understand what they probably have, whether it is urgent, and book a consultation (by phone at Clínica Cardiorreal).

## Product Purpose

The website of Dr. David García, specialist in Angiología y Cirugía Vascular y Endovascular in Ciudad Real, who sees patients at Clínica Cardiorreal (Calle Alonso Céspedes Guzmán, 10, Ciudad Real; no private practice of his own for now). It must rank in local search (Google, maps, AI search) for vascular conditions in Ciudad Real and turn visits into calls to the clinic. Success: phone bookings attributed to web and Google; top-3 local positions for the core queries.

## Positioning

- Board-certified specialist (MIR) in Angiología y Cirugía Vascular y Endovascular, the same vascular surgeon who works at the Hospital General Universitario de Ciudad Real. Not a commercial chain.
- Covers the full range: minimally invasive techniques (endovenous ablation, sclerotherapy/foam, endovascular procedures) and complex open surgery. Diagnoses and treats; operates what he diagnoses.
- Focus areas: chronic venous insufficiency (varices) and pelvic venous congestion.
- Eco-doppler performed by the surgeon himself in the same visit: diagnosis and plan in one consultation.
- Explains clearly: known science communicator (@davidgarmd).

## Operating Context

- Local private consultation in Ciudad Real capital; patients from the whole province.
- Booking by phone through Clínica Cardiorreal reception. WhatsApp, email and online booking are optional and appear only when filled in.
- Spanish healthcare advertising rules apply (RD 1907/1996, Castilla-La Mancha health-centre registry number, medical deontology code): no cure promises, no testimonials presented as results, no comparative claims naming competitors, no bait pricing.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Conditions covered: varices/chronic venous insufficiency, pelvic venous congestion, peripheral arterial disease, diabetic foot, abdominal aortic aneurysm, deep vein thrombosis (follow-up), leg ulcers, carotid stenosis, vascular eco-doppler.
- Pending real data (placeholders in `contenido.py`): phone, address, centre name, collegiate number, health-registry number, hours, exact coordinates, domain, surname, CV details.
- No cookies, no third-party trackers; fonts self-hosted.

## Brand Commitments

- Personal brand @davidgarmd, patient line "Vascular para Pacientes": palette navy `#0A2463`, light blue `#56CCF2`, white; red `#EB5757` only for alarm/urgent signals; green `#27AE60` only for healthy habits/reassurance. Sans-serif (Inter/Montserrat), extra-bold titles, generous sizes for older readers.
- Illustration style validated by David: warm flat editorial vector illustration, recognisable human scenes, vessels always drawn as open tubes with through-flow, no gore, no surgical photos, no real wounds. Fixed prompt in the vascularpacientes-visuales skill.
- Voice: second person ("tú"), everyday analogies for every medical term, myths corrected kindly, one clear urgency criterion with explicit action, close on one concrete behaviour, educational disclaimer. The surgeon appears in third person on condition pages. No em-dashes.
- Slogan: "Cirugía vascular, ciencia y preguntas incómodas: medicina bien contada".

## Evidence on Hand

- Real: specialist title, hospital affiliation, Instagram @davidgarmd, patient-education content line.
- Absent and must not be fabricated: patient testimonials, review ratings, case numbers, outcome statistics, prices, photos of the doctor or the consultation (to be provided by David), awards.

## Product Principles

1. Tell the patient what they probably have and whether it is urgent before asking them to book.
2. Specialist authority shown through clarity and precision, never through hype or promises.
3. Urgency is unmistakable: red means "go to A&E today", nothing else is red.
4. Every page earns local search on its own condition; no doorway pages.
5. Fast and legible on an old phone for a 70-year-old reader.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Older audience: base text 18px or more, WCAG AA contrast minimum, large touch targets (44px+), visible focus, reduced motion respected, plain language.
